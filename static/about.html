Take your work further

Export GLB for colour and material appearance, or STL for geometry. Inspect the mesh before physical production.

Your first test

  1. Open Workspace and select Single image.
  2. Upload a photo, choose an available engine and start with High quality.
  3. Generate, then check the front, sides and inferred back.
  4. Compare the silhouette, proportions and colour with the original.
  5. Export the model and open it in your preferred 3D viewer.

Single-image generation estimates unseen surfaces. A successful mesh check does not establish real-world dimensions or exact photographic accuracy. Hardware capture and calibration are separate workflows. Some advanced engines and social sign-in require additional setup.
